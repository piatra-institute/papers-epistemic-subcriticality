## Verified but not cited

The seed's bibliography is larger than the paper's, and the following were checked and left out because the paper does not engage them. Leaving a verified source uncited is preferable to a decorative citation.

- Goldman (1999) *Knowledge in a Social World* and Goldman (1991) on epistemic paternalism; Nguyen (2020) on echo chambers; Zollman (2007) on communication structure and Zollman (2009) on transient diversity; Kitcher (1990) on the division of cognitive labour. All verified. The paper's argument runs through the formal object rather than through veritistic social epistemology, and a paragraph of names would not have changed it.
- Piovarchy and Siskind (2023) on epistemic health and immunity; Ecker, Spearing and DiResta (2026) on misinformation as strategy; Harambam and Aupers (2015) on contested epistemic authority; Douglas et al. (2019) on conspiracy belief; Jasanoff (2003), Collins and Evans (2002), Rose (1985), Bisin and Verdier (2001), Huang, Jia and Yu (2024). All verified against Crossref.

## Structured bibliography (2026-09-23)

The inline reference list was migrated to references.yaml (CSL) and in-text citations to Pandoc [@id] syntax. Every entry now carries a DOI and was resolved through doi.org content negotiation (17 matched automatically in Crossref; costello2024, diekmann2010, fricker2007, hoes2024, marshall1984, paolillo2018, polletta2001, sperber1985, thorp2026, vine1963 and watts2002 were matched by hand to their DOI records). Missing fields were completed from the records (Lakatos and Musgrave as editors of the 1970 volume; page ranges for Sperber and Taylor; article numbers for Brooks and Porter, Costello et al. and Yee). Source claims in claims.yaml were checked against abstracts retrieved from Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar, and for Bettencourt et al. against the arXiv full text.
The DOI records for hoes2024 and lu2023 list five authors each (Hoes, Aitken, Zhang, Gackowski and Wojcieszak; Lu, Hu, Li, Bi and Ju); the legacy entries gave only the first three. The structured entries follow the records, and the rendered citations are "Hoes et al." and "Lu et al.". Crossref records thorp2026 as an expression of concern attached to costello2024.
